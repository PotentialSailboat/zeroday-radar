About / How to use

ZeroDay Radar is a public, open-source triage tool for security analysts and SOC teams. Start with new KEV additions and the highest-risk records, filter by CVE, vendor or product, verify each advisory, and compare it with your organisation’s asset inventory before acting. RSS shows observed changes, not a guarantee of new vulnerabilities in every feed.

For implementation and caveats, read the project README. Alerts support newly observed KEV records and large EPSS increases; PoC and remediation alerts remain planned. Read the alert history and subscription guide.

Understanding the signals

KEV means CISA has evidence of exploitation in the wild. EPSS estimates the probability of exploitation in the next 30 days; it is not evidence of exploitation. CVSS reflects technical severity, not observed exploitation. Missing NVD severity is shown as unavailable, not zero.

The main priority table covers CISA KEV. The emerging-discovery collection is bounded and may be incomplete; high EPSS is a prediction, not evidence of exploitation. No prioritisation accounts for your actual assets or exposure. Priorities are heuristic triage labels, not an asset-aware risk score. Validate applicability and exposure before remediation decisions.
