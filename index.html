Understanding the signals

KEV means CISA has evidence of exploitation in the wild. EPSS estimates the probability of exploitation in the next 30 days; it is not evidence of exploitation. CVSS reflects technical severity, not observed exploitation. Missing NVD severity is shown as unavailable, not zero.

Coverage in v0.1 is limited to the CISA KEV catalogue. Priorities are heuristic triage labels, not an asset-aware risk score. Validate applicability and exposure before remediation decisions.
